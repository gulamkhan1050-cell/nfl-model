How a prediction is made

Each team carries an Elo rating, smoothed EPA per play for pass and rush offence and defence (from play-by-play), success rates, points for and against, last-5 form and a rating for its starting quarterback (offensive EPA under him, adjusted for the defences he faced). Rest days, short weeks, division games, playoffs, neutral sites, dome, temperature, wind and surface are added, plus the injury report (each Out/Doubtful/Questionable player weighted by position and by his actual snap share from the latest snap-count data, falling back to his depth-chart rank, offence and defence separately, trained on every report since 2015), travel distance, time-zone shift and early body-clock kickoffs, altitude, and a new-head-coach flag. Weather comes from the Open-Meteo forecast for the stadium at kickoff. Three models are fitted on every game since 2015: a logistic model for the winner, a regression for the margin (spread) and a regression for total points. Each is then blended with the consensus bookmaker line using weights fitted on past seasons, and the residual spread of past errors gives cover and over probabilities at every book's line. Edges are shown per book; 4%+ is meaningful, under 2% is noise. Every game you load is recorded so the Scorecard can show how the model actually does against the closing lines.
